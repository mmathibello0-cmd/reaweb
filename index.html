From a distance

I don't want to pretend this is a goodbye. I don't want to sign off with some beautiful ending and act like I'm ready to walk away, because I'm not. The truth is, I still want you. I still want us. I still want to talk to you, hear your voice, laugh with you, and have the kind of conversations where neither of us has to think about what to say next. I know things aren't the way they used to be. I know you've made your choice, and I know I can't make that choice for you. But I also don't want to lie and say I don't wish you would choose differently. I do. I wish you would answer me. I wish we could talk. I wish we could sit down and be completely honest with each other about everything that happened, everything that hurt, and everything that still remains.

Maybe that's selfish. Maybe it's vulnerable. Maybe it's me admitting that I haven't figured out how to let go.

But this is me being honest. I don't want to leave.
